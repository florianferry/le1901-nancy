Heritage & Design

The Soul of Le 1901

Le 1901 is more than a charming rental; it is a tribute to the School of Nancy and the Art Nouveau movement that defined the city at the turn of the 20th century. Located in the historic Charles III district, the apartment was meticulously renovated in 2026 to preserve its architectural soul while integrating modern comfort. The interior design features custom backlit arches, massive oak floors, and authentic decorative elements inspired by artists like Majorelle and Gallé, including original 1910 Art Nouveau light fixtures. Residents enjoy a unique blend of heritage aesthetics—such as the high ceilings and large windows common in Belle Époque townhouses—and modern amenities like ultra-fast fibre optics and an OLED smart entertainment system. In the shower room, the floor, the wall and the shower tray are heated. The bed linen is cotton percale, all 5 windows are reinforced double glazing, and a lift runs to the second floor.

What that changes on a stay of several days. Near Place Stanislas Where to stay near Place Stanislas, six minutes on foot Le 1901 is 500 m from Place Stanislas, a six-minute walk north. That is about as close as you can sleep: not one of the square’s four sides holds an apartment, and the only hotel on it is closed for renovation. Art Nouveau Art Nouveau in Nancy, and whether the trip is worth it Yes, if Art Nouveau is what you are after. Nancy holds two museums, one villa and a run of façades left behind by a group of manufacturers who joined forces in February 1901 around Émile Gallé. The École de Nancy museum and Villa Majorelle share a single ticket at €9. Everything else you read straight off the sidewalk, at no cost. Getting here How to get to Nancy and where to park Nancy-Ville station is a 10-minute walk from Le 1901, with direct TGV trains to Paris-Est in about 1 hr 30. If you drive, the A31 puts Metz around 50 minutes away and Luxembourg around 1 hr 20, and the Saint-Dizier and Dom Calmet parking garages sit under 3 minutes from the door. Self check-in starts at 4 p.m. with no cut-off hour. From Paris Nancy from Paris: a day trip, or a night on the spot Paris-Est to Nancy-Ville runs with no intermediate stop in about 1 hr 30, with roughly nine round trips a day. The day trip works: take away the three hours on the train and you keep 7 to 9 hours in town, enough for Place Stanislas, the fine arts museum and the medieval old town. It is not enough for the École de Nancy, the Art Nouveau half of the city, whose two sites sit across town and close at 6 p.m. One night changes the trip. Saint Nicholas and Christmas Saint Nicholas and Christmas markets in Nancy: 2026 dates and where to stay The Saint Nicholas and end-of-year celebrations run from 20 November 2026 to 3 January 2027, the Christmas markets open on 22 November, and the weekend to aim for is 5 and 6 December 2026: the main parade crosses the historic center on Saturday 5 December at 6 p.m. and ends on Place Stanislas, with Saint Nicholas’ day itself falling on Sunday 6 December. Le 1901 sits 500 m from the square, a six-minute walk up Rue Saint-Dizier. Nancy with children Nancy with children, from a 50 m² apartment Le 1901 sleeps four in two bedrooms, on the second floor with an elevator. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I arrive late?

Arrival is possible at any time from 4:00 PM (self-check-in) thanks to an intelligent connected lock allowing full autonomy.

Where can I park my car?

We recommend the Saint-Dizier or Dom Calmet secure car parks, located less than 3 minutes away on foot. Street parking is possible but is charged during the day.

Is the apartment quiet?

Rest is guaranteed by high-performance sound insulation on the 5 double-glazed windows, despite the privileged location in the heart of the city.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are welcome at Le 1901. We simply ask that you ensure the premises are clean when you leave.

Can I cook on-site?

A fully equipped high-end kitchen is at your disposal, including oven, induction, microwave, and a large fridge/freezer combo.

What is the maximum guest capacity?

Le 1901 can accommodate up to 4 guests with its 160cm main bed and a high-end 140cm fold-away bed located in the office area.

Are linens and towels provided?

Absolutely. We provide cotton percale bed linens and hotel-quality towels for your maximum comfort.

Is the apartment accessible by elevator?

Yes, the apartment is located on the 2nd floor of a historic building and is fully accessible by elevator.

Do you have baby equipment?

Le 1901 is baby-friendly: we provide a travel cot, a high chair, and suitable cutlery for your children on-site.

How to get to Le 1901 from the station?

Nancy TGV station is about a 10-minute walk away. You can also reach the city centre in 2 tram or bus stops.

What is the quality of the internet connection?

You have an ultra-fast Fibre Optique connection accessible via Wifi 6 or Ethernet cable for maximum stability.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 4:00 PM (self-check-in) and check-out is before 11:00 AM, to allow us to prepare the apartment with care.

Is the tourist tax included in the price?

No. The tourist tax is added to the nightly rate, along with the cleaning fee and the service fee. It is collected by Cocoonr, our concierge partner, on behalf of the Métropole du Grand Nancy, and its amount appears in the quote breakdown before any payment.

What am I committing to when I book?

Your booking is confirmed straight away, with no deposit: you only pay at the point set by Cocoonr, before arrival. Cancellation is free up to 7 days before the stay begins. No security deposit is required. We only ask that you do not smoke in the flat, keep the noise down after 10 pm out of respect for the building, and leave the place clean when you go. Pets are welcome.
