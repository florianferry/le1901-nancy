Practical Information

Frequently Asked Questions

What am I committing to when I book?

Your booking is confirmed straight away, with no deposit: you only pay at the point set by Cocoonr, before arrival. Cancellation is free up to 7 days before the stay begins. No security deposit is required. We only ask that you do not smoke in the flat, keep the noise down after 10 pm out of respect for the building, and leave the place clean when you go. Pets are welcome.

Is the tourist tax included in the price?

No. The tourist tax is added to the nightly rate, along with the cleaning fee and the service fee. It is collected by Cocoonr, our concierge partner, on behalf of the Métropole du Grand Nancy, and its amount appears in the quote breakdown before any payment. Because the apartment is officially rated as a 3-star furnished tourist rental, it is charged at a fixed rate rather than a percentage of the nightly price: €1.35 per adult per night in 2026, so €5.40 for two adults staying two nights. Guests under 18 pay nothing.

Can I arrive late?

Yes, from 4 p.m. and at any hour after that. The lock is a smart lock: your code is sent to you before arrival, and nobody has to meet you there.

What is the maximum guest capacity?

Four guests: a 160 cm bed in the main bedroom and a 140 cm fold-away bed in the second bedroom, which doubles as a work space.

Where can I park my car?

We recommend the Saint-Dizier or Dom Calmet secure car parks, located less than 5 minutes away on foot. Street parking is possible but is charged during the day.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are welcome at Le 1901. We simply ask that you ensure the premises are clean when you leave.

Is the apartment quiet?

Not entirely. The flat faces a busy city-centre street: the 5 double-glazed windows soften the traffic noise but do not remove it. If you are a light sleeper, bring earplugs.

Can I cook on-site?

Yes. Oven, induction hob, microwave, dishwasher, fridge-freezer, kettle, toaster and a Nespresso machine.

Are linens and towels provided?

Yes. Cotton percale bed linen and bath towels are provided at no extra charge.

Is the apartment accessible by elevator?

Yes, the apartment is located on the 2nd floor of a historic building and is fully accessible by elevator.

Do you have baby equipment?

Le 1901 is baby-friendly: we provide a travel cot, a high chair, and suitable cutlery for your children on-site.

How to get to Le 1901 from the station?

Nancy TGV station is about a 13-minute walk away. You can also reach the city centre in 2 tram or bus stops.

What is the quality of the internet connection?

Fibre optic, over Wi-Fi 6 or by Ethernet cable. Wired Ethernet sockets are fitted in the second bedroom, at the work space.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 4:00 PM (self-check-in) and check-out is before 11:00 AM, to allow us to prepare the apartment with care.

Heritage & Design

Renovated in the spirit of the École de Nancy

Le 1901 occupies a flat in the Charles III district, renovated in 2026. The brief was the École de Nancy: custom backlit arches, solid oak floors, glazed doors with floral motifs, and original 1910 Art Nouveau wall lights put back into service. The high ceilings and five tall windows come with the building, not with the decoration. Everything else is contemporary: fibre optic internet and wired Ethernet sockets, an OLED TV, automated inertia heating. In the shower room, the floor, the wall and the shower tray are heated. The bed linen is cotton percale, all 5 windows are reinforced double glazing, and a lift runs to the second floor.

Renovated in 2026 by its owner, Florian Ferry, who documents renovation work on flo-perspectives.com.

The Maison des Sœurs Macarons on rue Gambetta sells the macaron de Nancy: almonds, sugar and egg white, no colouring, from a late-18th-century recipe.

The cradle of Art Nouveau in Nancy. Furniture, glassware and ceramics by Gallé, Daum and Majorelle in a period villa.

Villa Majorelle

The first fully Art Nouveau house in Nancy, built in 1901 by Henri Sauvage for Louis Majorelle. Ironwork by Majorelle, stained glass by Jacques Gruber.

Visit

Place Stanislas

A royal square completed in 1755, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1983. Gilded gates by Jean Lamour, fountains by Barthélémy Guibal.

Parc de la Pépinière

21 hectares of greenery in the city centre. Rose garden, small animal park and shaded walkways: Nancy's green lung, 12 min walk from Le 1901.
