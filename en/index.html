Heritage & Design

The Soul of Le 1901

Le 1901 is more than a charming rental; it is a tribute to the School of Nancy and the Art Nouveau movement that defined the city at the turn of the 20th century. Located in the historic Charles III district, the apartment was meticulously renovated in 2026 to preserve its architectural soul while integrating modern comfort. The interior design features custom backlit arches, massive oak floors, and authentic decorative elements inspired by artists like Majorelle and Gallé, including original 1910 Art Nouveau light fixtures. Residents enjoy a unique blend of heritage aesthetics—such as the high ceilings and large windows common in Belle Époque townhouses—and modern amenities like ultra-fast fibre optics and an OLED smart entertainment system. Exceptional thermal comfort is provided by underfloor heating in the bathroom and the shower tray. This strategic balance ensures that guests experience the prestigious atmosphere of 1900s Nancy without compromising on the comfort and charm required by today's travellers. Every detail, from the percale linens to the reinforced acoustic insulation, has been curated to provide a quiet sanctuary in the hyper-centre of Nancy.

Practical Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I arrive late?

Arrival is possible at any time from 4:00 PM (self-check-in) thanks to an intelligent connected lock allowing full autonomy.

Where can I park my car?

We recommend the Saint-Dizier or Dom Calmet secure car parks, located less than 3 minutes away on foot. Street parking is possible but is charged during the day.

Is the apartment quiet?

Rest is guaranteed by high-performance sound insulation on the 5 double-glazed windows, despite the privileged location in the heart of the city.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are welcome at Le 1901. We simply ask that you ensure the premises are clean when you leave.

Can I cook on-site?

A fully equipped high-end kitchen is at your disposal, including oven, induction, microwave, and a large fridge/freezer combo.

What is the maximum guest capacity?

Le 1901 can accommodate up to 4 guests with its 160cm main bed and a high-end 140cm fold-away bed located in the office area.

Are linens and towels provided?

Absolutely. We provide cotton percale bed linens and hotel-quality towels for your maximum comfort.

Is the apartment accessible by elevator?

Yes, the apartment is located on the 2nd floor of a historic building and is fully accessible by elevator.

Do you have baby equipment?

Le 1901 is baby-friendly: we provide a travel cot, a high chair, and suitable cutlery for your children on-site.

How to get to Le 1901 from the station?

Nancy TGV station is about a 10-minute walk away. You can also reach the city centre in 2 tram or bus stops.

What is the quality of the internet connection?

You have an ultra-fast Fibre Optique connection accessible via Wifi 6 or Ethernet cable for maximum stability.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 4:00 PM (self-check-in) and check-out is before 11:00 AM, to allow us to prepare the apartment with care.

Is the tourist tax included in the price?

No. The tourist tax is added to the nightly rate, along with the cleaning fee and the service fee. It is collected by Cocoonr, our concierge partner, on behalf of the Métropole du Grand Nancy, and its amount appears in the quote breakdown before any payment.
